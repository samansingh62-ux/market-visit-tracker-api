Sign in

Log in with the same username and PIN as the app. Send the token as Authorization: Bearer <token> on every call. Managers can use X-API-Key instead. Zone heads with zone view add X-View: zone to read all of Zone A.

curl -X POST https://market-visit-tracker-api.onrender.com/auth/login \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"username":"YOUR_NAME","password":"YOUR_PIN"}'
# → {"access_token":"…","user":{"name":"…","role":"TL"}}

TOKEN=…   # keep it private: it works like your PIN
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" https://market-visit-tracker-api.onrender.com/performance-dashboard

Who sees what

LoginData returned
Admin, CEO, Manager, Zone A heads (zone view)All of Zone A
KAMTheir own area (all TLs, SSs and outlets in it)
SSTheir TLs and all outlets (PO and NPO) under them
TLTheir own PO outlets only
RDSOutlets billed through that RDS

Scoping is done on the server. The same call returns different rows for different logins; there is no parameter to widen it.

Performance

GET/performance-dashboardAll logins

Month-to-date sales vs target for every outlet in your area, today’s sale (FTD), stock, days of stock, last visit, and scheme payout per outlet. Includes hierarchy.tl, hierarchy.ss, hierarchy.rds totals, which the TL-wise FTD page uses (ftd_sales, ftd_counters, kam, ss on each TL).

GET/my-target-performanceAll logins

Your own target, achievement and per-day need.

Review cards, image & PPT

GET/review?role=TL&name=Monojit Rudra PaulTL own · SS own TLs · KAM area · Admin all

One-page review as JSON: summary (MTD, target, gap, need per day, FTD), flags, TLs under the person, every counter with stock, days of stock and last visit, and a 5-point action plan.

role TL, SS, KAM or RDS · name the person’s name as in the mapping. A KAM or SS opening an RDS sees only their part of it (limited_to).

GET/review/image?role=…&name=…Same as /review

The review card as a PNG file (1080 px wide), ready for WhatsApp.

curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" -OJ "https://market-visit-tracker-api.onrender.com/review/image?role=TL&name=Monojit%20Rudra%20Paul"
GET/review/ppt?role=…&name=…Same as /review

The review as a PowerPoint deck (.pptx): title, month so far, TL performance with chart, counters, stock and visits, action plan.

Schemes & stock payout

GET/performance-dashboardAll logins

Each outlet carries schemes: Booster, Focus, Clearance, Additional (per series: units sold, slab, rate, payout, next slab), Back Support margin and shop-boy slabs. While the model slabs are open, each series also has stock and if_all_sold (slab, payout and extra if all stock on hand sells), and the outlet has stock_extra. Chain retailers carry shared and are counted once.

Focus models

GET/focus-modelsAll logins

Target vs achievement for this month’s focus variants in your area. Add ?variant=… for counters with stock but no sale, counters with a target but no stock, and VBAs at zero MTD and FTD.

Punched sales

GET/punched-salesAdmin · KAM · SS · TL

Sales punched by each VBA / RVBA / FVBA against their target, and units TLs and SSs punched under their own login. From the latest tertiary dashboard (date in as_of).

POST/admin/tertiary-dashboardAdmin · CEO · Manager

Upload the Tertiary Sales Dashboard .xlsx (multipart field file) to refresh punched sales. IMEIs are not stored.

Visits & visit report

POST/visitsField logins

Log a market visit (retailer, feedback, follow-up, GPS, optional V-Work details). Photos: POST /visits/{id}/photos.

GET/visitsAll logins

Visits in your area.

GET/visit-report?date_from=2026-11-01&date_to=2026-11-16Admin · Zone A heads · KAM

Outlets each TL, SS and KAM visited, with remarks, and TL-wise outlets per day. Up to two months per call.

GET/my-visits/vwork-pendingField logins

Your visits from the last 7 days not yet ticked as logged in V-Work. Tick one with POST /visits/{id}/vwork-logged.

Stock finder

GET/stock/finder?model=Y11 5G&days=30TL · SS · KAM · zone

Stock at each counter against what it sold in the last 15/30/45/60 days: not-moving and slow stock, colours, and counters selling the variant but low on stock. Leave out model for the summary by model.

Retailer 360

GET/retailers/{code}/360Outlets in your area

One outlet: sales trend, model mix, stock by model, visits, competition and schemes.

GET/retailers/nearby?lat=…&lng=…Field logins

Outlets near a GPS point, used when logging a visit.

WhatsApp / Claude tools

The same data is available as tools over MCP at /mcp/ (Streamable HTTP, Authorization: Bearer <WHATSAPP_MCP_API_KEY>). Read tools return data for all of Zone A; send tools stay behind the WhatsApp delivery gates.

ToolWhat it returns
get_reviewReview of a TL, SS, KAM or RDS (summary, flags, counters, action plan)
get_tl_ftdToday’s sale by TL against the daily need
get_scheme_payoutScheme payout for a retailer, with stock on hand and the payout if it is all sold
get_focus_modelsFocus-model target vs achievement; with a variant, counters not moving it and VBAs at zero
get_punched_salesVBA / FVBA punched sales and TL & SS own punching
get_visit_reportOutlets visited and remarks by person, and TL day by day
get_whatsapp_status, list_templates, send_template_message, send_text_message, send_tl_broadcastWhatsApp status and sending (gated; see WHATSAPP_MCP.md)

Data comes from V-Work live sales and stock, the November mapping and target files, and the latest uploaded dashboards. Scheme figures are provisional.
